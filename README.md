## About me

- 🇨🇿 Freelance web developer and digital marketer from the Czech Republic, working as **spacekf**
- 🏗️ I take projects end to end: design, development, CMS, hosting, and the marketing that brings traffic in
- 🤝 Most of my clients are local businesses: tradespeople, photographers, shops and service companies
- 🎓 Running my freelance business while finishing secondary school

## Selected work

> Client names are left out on purpose. Happy to show live projects on a call.

- **Motorsport experience booking site.** Full rebuild from WordPress to Next.js + Payload, with a CMS the team runs on its own.
- **Photographer portfolios.** Image-heavy galleries served from a CDN, fast on mobile, easy to update.
- **Sales team app.** Shared inbox, rep profiles and an admin overview for a team of cold callers.

- **3D product configurator.** Interactive carport builder with live price calculation.
